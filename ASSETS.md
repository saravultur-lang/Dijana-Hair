# Fotografije i fontovi

Fotografije salona dostavio je korisnik. Sačuvane su kao optimizovani WebP fajlovi bez kreativnih izmjena: `salon-ulaz.webp`, `salon-recepcija.webp`, `salon-detalji.webp`, `salon-ogledala.webp`, `salon-enterijer.webp`.

Fotografije frizura su obrađene ugrađenim **Imagegen** alatom, zatim optimizovane u WebP. Izvorni fajlovi nisu prepisani. Obrada ne zamjenjuje pregled vlasnika da fotografije vjerno predstavljaju radove.

## Svečana frizura — `assets/svecana-frizura.webp`

Izvor: `Snimak ekrana 2026-11-10 151437.png`.

Korišteni prompt:

> Use case: identity-preserve. Edit target: the supplied photo of a woman in a black formal dress with a blonde updo. Asset: portrait photograph for DIJANA HAIR salon portfolio, black and muted gold website. Professionally retouch this exact photograph: improve clarity and hair detail, correct exposure and color, soft warm editorial lighting, natural skin texture. Keep the SAME woman's identity, profile, body, pose, black dress, exact hairstyle and hair color, bun shape and loose strands unchanged; this must faithfully represent the existing hairstyle. Remove the distracting white event seating chart and event text in background and replace with softly blurred charcoal interior with subtle warm bokeh, realistic optical depth of field. Portrait crop from above hair to waist, hair clearly visible with space above, no text or logos. Photorealistic premium beauty photography, restrained retouch, no invented hairstyle or accessories.

## Muška frizura — `assets/muska-frizura.webp`

Izvor: `ChatGPT Image Nov 5, 2026, 03_05_55 PM-1.png`.

Korišteni prompt:

> Use case: identity-preserve. Edit target: supplied rear portrait of a man's textured haircut with taper fade and double curved shaved lines, blue sweater. Asset: professional barber portfolio photo for black and gold DIJANA HAIR website. Enhance only photographic quality: refined sharp focus on exact haircut and fine hair texture, restrained warm professional lighting, subtle neutral warm color grade, clean noise. Preserve exact haircut silhouette, fade, two curved shaved lines, every hair design detail, head shape, pose, skin texture, sweater and framing. Keep softly defocused salon background darker charcoal with restrained warm golden bokeh. Do not invent or change the haircut, no extra styling, no text, no watermark. Natural authentic photographic retouch.
